Spain: deadline to appeal an asylum refusal

The deadlines below are for appealing an asylum refusal in Spain. The exact deadline is written at the bottom of your decision; always follow it.

In Spain, you may first lodge an optional administrative request for reconsideration (recurso de reposición) against a refusal with the Ministry of the Interior (OAR / Ministerio del Interior). The judicial route is an action before the administrative chamber of the National High Court (Audiencia Nacional, Sala de lo Contencioso-Administrativo); there you must be represented by a lawyer (abogado) and give power of attorney to a procurador. No appeal automatically stops the decision from being carried out; for that you must separately request an interim measure. If you meet the income test, you can receive free legal aid (asistencia jurídica gratuita) at both the administrative and the court stage. Because no national implementing law has been adopted yet, applications made from 12 June 2026 are processed under the EU regulations, following an instruction of the Ministry of the Interior.

Who can file the appeal?

In this country the appeal is, as a rule, filed by a lawyer; you cannot file it yourself. Ask for a free lawyer (legal aid) at once. We can prepare the appeal text for your lawyer.
