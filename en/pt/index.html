Portugal: deadline to appeal an asylum refusal

The deadlines below are for appealing an asylum refusal in Portugal. The exact deadline is written at the bottom of your decision; always follow it.

In Portugal, the first decision on an asylum application is made by AIMA. An appeal against this decision is lodged with the administrative court (Tribunal Administrativo); this judicial review is treated as urgent and follows a simplified procedure. At the appeal stage you are entitled to free legal aid; it depends on an income assessment, and the lawyer is appointed by the Bar. The procedures regulation of the EU Pact on Migration and Asylum has applied directly since 12 June 2026; Portugal regulated the application of the Pact regulations through Lei n.º 62/2026 of 10 September 2026, which also amended the asylum law, Lei 27/2008.
