What do the words in your asylum refusal mean?

We explain 8 words used in German and Dutch asylum decisions in your language. Each word's page gives the appeal deadline, whether you are protected from removal, and the next steps.

Germany

offensichtlich unbegründet
Your application was examined and rejected as “manifestly unfounded” (AsylG § 30). This is harsher than a normal rejection (unbegründet): the deadlines are short and the court case does not protect you from deportation by itself.
unzulässig
Your application was not examined on its merits; it was found “inadmissible” (AsylG § 29). This happens, for example, when another EU country has already given you protection, when there is a safe third country or first country of asylum for you, or when a repeat application brings no new facts.
Abschiebungsanordnung
This is a deportation order: Germany will not examine your application itself and wants to send you to another country, meaning another EU country responsible for your application or a safe third country (AsylG § 34a).
unbegründet
Your application was examined and rejected as “unfounded”. If the word “offensichtlich” (manifestly) is not there, this is the normal rejection: the deadline is longer and as a rule you stay in Germany while the court case runs.

Netherlands

kennelijk ongegrond
Your application was examined and rejected as “manifestly unfounded” (Vw 2000 art. 30b). This is harsher than a normal rejection (ongegrond): the deadlines are short and the beroep does not protect you from deportation by itself.
niet-ontvankelijk
Your application was not examined on its merits; it was found “inadmissible” (Vw 2000 art. 30a). This happens, for example, when another EU country has already given you protection, when there is a safe third country for you, or when a repeat application brings no new facts.
ongegrond
Your application was examined and rejected as “unfounded” (Vw 2000 art. 31). If the word “kennelijk” (manifestly) is not there, this is the normal rejection: the deadline is longer and as a rule you stay in the Netherlands while the beroep runs.
overdrachtsbesluit
This is a transfer decision: the Netherlands will not examine your application itself and wants to send you to another EU country responsible for it (formerly called “Dublin”; now EU Regulation 2024/1351, Vw 2000 art. 62b).

This is an estimate and deliberately shows the EARLIEST possible day. Your exact deadline is stated at the end of your decision; always follow the deadline in your decision. Some countries add extra days for decisions sent by post; do not rely on them.
