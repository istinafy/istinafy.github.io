Poland: deadline to appeal an asylum refusal

The deadlines below are for appealing an asylum refusal in Poland. The exact deadline is written at the bottom of your decision; always follow it.

Decision typeAppeal deadline
Regular rejection14 days
Accelerated procedure7 days
Inadmissibility decision10 days
Dublin / transfer to another country14 days
Subsequent application10 days

Where and how to appeal

Appeal body
Rada do Spraw Uchodźców
Language of the procedure
Polish
When the deadline starts
From the day the decision is notified to you. To stay safe, count from the earlier of two dates: the date on the decision or the day it reached you.
If the last day falls on a weekend
No extension is confirmed: file on the working day before.
Next court (further appeal)
WSA w Warszawie
Price
Roadmap: Free · Appeal letter: 149 €

Medium

Who can file the appeal?

In this country you can usually file the first appeal yourself. At the higher court a lawyer is often required.
