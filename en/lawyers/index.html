Our working method

Every file follows the same rigorous path.

We tied the research and writing methods taught at law faculties into one 13-stage working sequence. Here it is in eight headings. No step is skipped; the output of each step is the input of the next.

01

Document review and chronology

The decision, judgment, statement of claim and every document in the file are read. Events are put in date order; each fact is classed as documented, statement only, contradictory or missing.

Case-solving method (casusmethode) · Hage, Verheij & Fernhout, Vaardig met recht
02

Procedure and deadline

Type of decision, procedure, competent court and deadline are established. The notice of appeal in the decision is compared with the statutory text; if they differ, we take the shorter period.

Awb · Rv · Sv · Vw 2000
03

Breaking down the grounds

Each ground of the decision, judgment or other side is treated as a separate legal question. No ground is left unanswered.

Requirements check · gronden and grieven
04

Primary source research

EU law, statutes, regulations, policy rules and case law are researched in their primary sources. The conclusion rests on the text of the provision and the ruling itself, not on a secondary summary.

Doctrinal legal research · Hutchinson & Duncan (2012)
05

Case law analysis

For each ruling the facts, holding and reasoning are separated; its similarity to and difference from our file are written down. Whether the ruling was later overturned is checked separately.

Systematic case law analysis · Peeraer & Van Gestel (2021)
06

Evidence and credibility

Each piece of evidence is weighed for relevance, reliability and probative value. In asylum files credibility is also tested against the four conditions of Article 4(5) of the EU Qualification Regulation.

Burden of proof and assessment of evidence · UNHCR, Beyond Proof (2013)
07

Reasoning and drafting

Conclusion first; then the rule, its application, the counter-argument and the answer. Clear, concise and confident legal language.

IRAC / CREAC · Feteris (2017)
08

Final review

The draft is reviewed in six layers and read by a second pair of eyes that did not write it.

Leidraad voor juridische auteurs (2025) · Groene Boekje
All 13 stages
  1. Document inventoryEvery document in the file is counted, named and dated.
  2. Status report and confirmationThe grounds of the decision and the state of the file are set out; open points are put to you.
  3. ProcedureType of decision, forum, remedy and deadline are calculated from the statutory provision.
  4. FactsEvents are put in date order and each fact is compared with its document.
  5. EvidenceEach piece of evidence is weighed for relevance, reliability and probative value.
  6. Legal researchStatute, EU law and case law are researched in their primary sources.
  7. IssuesEach ground is turned into a separate legal question.
  8. StrategyWhich argument is raised, and in what order, is decided.
  9. Missing informationInformation and documents that must be in place before drafting are listed.
  10. DraftingThe draft is written conclusion first, with rule and application.
  11. Review A–FSix-layer review; a second pair of eyes that did not write the text.
  12. Feedback loopEvery gap found in review is fixed and the review is run again.
  13. Delivery and lessonsThe package is uploaded to the portal; every lesson from the file is fed back into the method.

Six-layer review

Checked six times before delivery.

Each layer has its own checklist. No draft reaches you before every layer has passed.

A

Source

Every citation is verified at its official source; quotations are verbatim. Citation style follows the Leidraad voor juridische auteurs, case law by ECLI.

B

Facts and evidence

Every factual sentence rests on a document or statement in the file.

C

Procedure

Court, deadline, mandatory claims, court fee, annexes and signature block.

D

Argument

Every ground answered with facts, law and conclusion; the counter-argument addressed.

E

Language and style

Spelling is checked letter by letter against the Groene Boekje; names, dates and numbers are consistent; a clear style in line with the Klare taal principle.

F

Document layout

Page layout, numbering, index of annexes and a format ready to file.

Specialism

The Ankara Agreement, our specialism.

The rights of Turkish nationals and their families in the Netherlands rest on a separate body of law alongside general immigration law: Association law. In these files we examine every ground against this law first: the standstill clauses, the rights under Decision 1/80 and the case law of the EU Court of Justice. Whether a refusal took this law into account can decide the outcome of the file.

  1. 1963Association Agreement, Ankara
  2. 1970Additional Protocol
  3. 1980Decision No 1/80 of the Association Council

Standstill

Any new condition stricter than those in force when the association provisions took effect is prohibited. The exception is public policy or an overriding reason in the public interest; even then the measure must be suitable and go no further than necessary. The aim of preventing illegal entry does not on its own set the standstill aside.

art. 41 Additional Protocol · arts 13 and 14 Decision 1/80 · Demir, C-225/12 · Yön, C-123/17

Rights of Turkish workers

A Turkish worker who has worked lawfully for one year with the same employer acquires the right by law to keep working there and to stay; no separate decision is needed. After three years with the same employer no work permit (TWV) is required.

art. 6 Decision 1/80 · Vc B10/4 · IND

Family members

A family member who has lived with a Turkish worker for three years on a family permit acquires an independent right; the family member need not be Turkish. For a Turkish national working in the Netherlands and their spouse the age requirement is 18, not 21.

art. 7 Decision 1/80 · Vc B10/4 · IND

MVV requirement

Since 1 October 2022 the lack of an MVV is again an independent ground for refusal. The exemption requires four conditions together; special individual circumstances must be raised and proven in the application. Applications made before that date are not refused for lack of an MVV if the other conditions are met.

art. 3.71(2)(e) Vb · Vc B1/4.1.2 · ABRvS 1.7.2025

Self-employed persons

No points system applies to a Turkish national's self-employment application; RVO assesses only the business plan. A Turkish national who comes as self-employed is not subject to the civic integration (inburgering) requirement.

art. 41 Additional Protocol · Tum and Dari, C-16/05 · ABRvS 1.7.2025 · IND

Fees

A Turkish national may not be charged a fee disproportionate to what an EU citizen pays for a similar document. In the 2026 tariff an Association work or self-employment application costs €85; a regular self-employment application €423.

Commission v Netherlands, C-92/07 · IND fee tariff 2026

Questions

What lawyers ask.

Is the draft really usable?

The page above comes from an anonymised sample file; our deliveries are at this level. The best proof is still your own file: your first file is free.

Are the citations correct?

Every ruling and provision is verified at its official source, with the check date; we also check whether the ruling was later overturned. No citation that is not in the source list enters the text.

Who prepares the draft?

We run our method with software we developed ourselves; every draft is shaped by our method and the six-layer review. A person reads the text before delivery.

How good is the Dutch?

The draft is written in Dutch. Spelling is checked letter by letter against the Groene Boekje; the style is clear and confident, in line with the courts' Klare taal principle.

Which documents should I upload?

The decision or judgment, the other side's document and the annexes in the file. Add your own note if you have one. If we see a document missing, we ask you before we start writing.

What if the deadline is too close?

We state the delivery time when you order, based on the deadline and our capacity. If we miss the time we gave, the file is free.

How do you calculate the deadline?

In every file we calculate the period from the statutory provision itself; for example art. 6:7 Awb for administrative objection and appeal, art. 408 Sv for criminal appeal, art. 339 Rv for civil appeal. We show you the calculation and the provision. If the notice in the decision differs from the statute, we take the shorter period.

Can I ask for corrections?

Corrections within the same scope are free within 7 days of delivery; the new version lands in the portal.

How do I pay?

An invoice per file, payable within 14 days; regular firms get one monthly invoice.

Who is responsible?

We prepare the draft; you read, edit, sign and file it. The lawyer always has the final word, and the workflow is built around that.

Which areas of law do you cover?

All 36 areas in which lawyers practise: criminal, family, employment, tenancy, contract and obligations, administrative, social security, tax, immigration and asylum law and the others. Every written document before Dutch courts and authorities.

What is different in Ankara Agreement files?

Association law is part of EU law and takes precedence over national rules: standstill, the rights under 1/80, the MVV exemption and fees follow their own rules. In these files we examine every ground against Association law first; we rely on the text of the rulings of the EU Court of Justice and the Council of State themselves.

What if it is my first file in this area?

We use the same method in every area. The procedure, deadlines and formal rules of an area sit in a separate procedure pack. If it is your first file in that area, we build and check the pack from official sources with your file, and we give the delivery time accordingly, before you order.
