The reality

In many cases the lawyer does not go to the higher court.

01

They see little chance and withdraw

After a refusal, many lawyers consider the case weak, do not appeal or withdraw. Often you only find out in the last days.

02

The deadline is very short

Since the Pact, the appeal period for accelerated decisions is only a few days. There is often no time to find a new lawyer.

03

Your right remains

In many countries you can file the appeal with your own signature. A lawyer not appealing does not take away your right to appeal.

If your lawyer has stopped, you do not have to. We prepare the appeal; you sign and send it.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

In which countries do you work?

In every country in Europe. The appeal is written in the language and under the rules of the court of the country that decided.

Which documents do I need?

Your rejection decision (all pages), your interview reports and any other documents you have. Write the decision date in your first message.

Who sends the appeal?

In most countries you do. The roadmap explains step by step to which court, to which address and how to send it. In Spain, Italy and Luxembourg your lawyer files the appeal; we prepare the text for them.

I have a lawyer, but they will not appeal. What now?

Your deadline keeps running. Send us the decision date on WhatsApp now and we will work out the deadline together.

What if my deadline has passed?

Write to us anyway, right away. If there was a valid reason for the delay, an appeal may still be possible.

How much does it cost, and when do I pay?

The roadmap is free. The appeal letter costs 149 €. You pay by bank transfer once your appeal letter is ready, after you have seen its first page; you can pay from any country in Europe.

Do you also write letters outside asylum and in other countries?

Yes. In Turkey, the Netherlands and every country in the world; we prepare letters to every authority and to courts at every level, for individuals, lawyers, companies and organisations.

My last day is very close; what should I do?

Write to us right away and give your last day in your first message. There is no rush fee. You pay once the appeal letter is ready; if your bank offers instant transfer, choose it, because a normal transfer can take one working day. The exact last day is always the one written in your decision.

Do I have to pay a court fee?

It depends on the country: in some, such as Romania, an asylum appeal is free of court fees; in others, such as Cyprus, a fee is charged. If there is a fee, it is paid directly to the court. Ask us on WhatsApp about your country's rule and we will tell you.

Are my documents safe?

This site has no forms and no uploads; you send your documents yourself, only by WhatsApp or email. We will never ask for passwords, verification codes or your ID number. Payment details come only from our official WhatsApp number; ignore any request from another number.

Legal basis (example: the Netherlands)

  • In the Netherlands, appeal deadlines against asylum decisions are set in the Aliens Act 2000 (Vreemdelingenwet), article 69.
  • In a further appeal to the Raad van State, the appeal letter must contain the grounds of appeal (grieven) against the first court ruling (Vreemdelingenwet, article 85).
  • To stop a removal, interim measures (voorlopige voorziening) can also be requested (General Administrative Law Act, Awb article 8:81).
  • Every court ruling (ECLI number) used in the appeal letter is checked one by one in the official sources (rechtspraak.nl, raadvanstate.nl).

The deadlines for other countries are on the country pages.
