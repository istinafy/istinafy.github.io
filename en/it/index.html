Italy: deadline to appeal an asylum refusal

The deadlines below are for appealing an asylum refusal in Italy. The exact deadline is written at the bottom of your decision; always follow it.

Decision typeAppeal deadline
Regular rejection30 days
Accelerated procedure7 days
Border procedure5 days

Where and how to appeal

Appeal body
Tribunale ordinario - sezione specializzata immigrazione
Language of the procedure
Italian
When the deadline starts
From the day the decision is notified to you. To stay safe, count from the earlier of two dates: the date on the decision or the day it reached you.
If the last day falls on a weekend
No extension is confirmed: file on the working day before.

Who can file the appeal?

In this country the appeal is, as a rule, filed by a lawyer; you cannot file it yourself. Ask for a free lawyer (legal aid) at once. We can prepare the appeal text for your lawyer.
